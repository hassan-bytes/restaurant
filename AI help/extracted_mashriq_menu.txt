MASHRIQ, meaning the East, is a celebration of warmth, 
hospitality, and flavours that bring people together.
Inspired by rich Eastern traditions and modern dining, we 
create food experiences that feel comforting, generous, and 
memorable. Every dish is prepared with honest ingredients, 
bold flavours, and thoughtful care.
More than just a place to dine, MASHRIQ is where families, 
friends, and food lovers come together to share good taste, 
meaningful moments, and great company.
Eastern dining, Arabic warmth & selected
international favourites
